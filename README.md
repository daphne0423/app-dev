# Captain America: Civil War

---

## A. Introduction

### Overview
*Captain America: Civil War* is one of my absolute favorite movie from the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU). it was released in the year **2016** and belongs to the *action, superhero, and sci-fi* genres. The story focuses on the big conflict between Captain America and Iron Man when the government tries to control the Avengers. It is an exciting film that shows how former friends can end up fighting for what they believe is right.

---

## B. Sypnosis
After a mission causes accidental damage, world governments decide to create the Sokovia Accords to monitor and control superheroes. Tony Stark (Iron Man) supports this law because he feels guilty about past disasters, but Steve Rogers (Captain America) believes heroes should stay free to help people without the government control. Things get much worse when Steve's best friend, Bucky Barnes, is framed for a bombing. Steve tries to protect Bucky, which causes the Avengers team to split into two opposing team. The situation leads to an epic battle between heroes at an airport and an emotional final fight between Cap and Iron Man.

> "He's my friend." - Steve Rogers
> "So was I." - Tony Stark

---

## C. Characters 

Five key characters 

* **Steve Rogers (Captain America)** - The leader who stands up against government control to protect his friend Bucky.
* **Tony Stark (Iron Man)** - A brilliant inventor who believes the Avengers need strict rules and accountability.
* **Bucky Barnes (The Winter Soldier)** - Cap's loyal best friend from the past who was brainwashed into an assassin.
* **T'Challa (Black Panther)** - A prince seeking revenge for his father's death, introducing a powerful new hero.
* **Peter Parker (Spider Man)** - A young hero recruited by Tony Stark who brings fun energy to the fight.

---

## D. Personal Review

### Why I Chose This Movie
I picked *Captain America: Civil  War* because it is not just a regular superhero movie where heroes fight a simple monster. Instead, it shows good people having different opinions, which make the  drama feel much more real and emotional.

### 3 Things I Liked About It
1. **The Airport Battle Scene** - It was amazing so many heroes fight against each other using their unique powers.
2. **Spider-Man's Introduction** - Peter Parker was funny and brought great humor to a tense story.
3. **The Emotional Ending** - The final fight felt personal and heart breaking because both Steve and Tony had understandable reasons for fighting.

### Rating and Recommendation
* **Personal Rating:** **10/10**
* **Recommendation:** I highly recommend this movie to anyone who likes superhero stories or action films. Even if  your not a huge Marvel fan, the action and emotional story make it totally  worth watching.
